  • 서비스 안정화 및 품질 개선: 앱 오류(Crash) 분석 및 안정적인 게임 환경 제공

Privacy Policy (English Version)

Leevelopment ("we", "us", or "our") built Janggi Arcade (package: com.Leevelopment.JanggiArcade) as a Free-to-Play ad-supported game. This page informs players regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

1. Information Collection & Use

We do NOT collect, store, or require any personally identifiable information (such as your real name, email, phone number, or physical address). The game does not operate any independent registration or login server.

However, the app utilizes third-party SDKs that may automatically collect certain device and advertising data to deliver advertisements:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device details: Device model, OS version, language, screen resolution
  • Diagnostic info: Crash logs and ad view metrics

2. Third-Party Services

The app links to services provided by third parties:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies Privacy Policy

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Game state and preferences are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app completely removes all local game data. Users can opt out of personalized ads or reset/delete their Advertising ID via Android system settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
